## Verified scope

TTCombat describes planet-bound fleet combat combining warships, strike craft and deployed ground forces. Its rulebook product page identifies the printed 2.0 rules. [Publisher rulebook description](https://ttcombat.com/products/dropfleet-commander-rulebook).

The publisher's resources page offers terrain/objective materials and labels its linked scenario collection unsupported. Do not treat those historical scenarios as proof of current rules. Exact layers, deployment, bombardment, scoring, movement and detection rules require a dedicated edition-specific review before transfer. [Publisher resources](https://ttcombat.com/pages/dropfleet-commander-downloads).

## Phoenix opportunities — proposals, not adopted mechanics

- Make success depend on useful work at planetary sites as well as ship destruction: deployment, supply, evacuation or securing access are candidate activities.
- Couple capital-ship positioning and protection to small-craft/team operations so several Stations contribute while an assignment runs.
- Compare protecting a carrier, covering a transfer and pursuing a hostile; a fleet victory need not automatically complete the surface objective.
- Reuse the selected shared facility/ship damage and team models. Do not import a separate tabletop ground battle or require on-foot human play.
