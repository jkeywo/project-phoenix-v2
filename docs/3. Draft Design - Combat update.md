# Combat Update

## Phaser Banks

- The ship has 2 phaser banks: port and starboard.
- Each has a 270° fire arc.
- Phasers can be set to **auto** or **manual** mode by the Weapons operator.
  - **Auto mode**: fires when the current target is within arc, cooldown is ready, and within effective range. Uses a conservative 240° arc for the fire decision (270° − 30° margin) to avoid wasted shots on targets clipping the edge. The phaser beam itself retains the full 270° arc.
  - **Manual mode**: Weapons operator presses a fire button.
- Phaser damage, colour, and cooldown are configurable in the ship entity config.
- Effective range for auto-fire is configurable separately from radar range.

## Torpedoes

- The ship has a limited supply of torpedoes (configurable, default 10).
- **Fore tubes**: 2 tubes, each with a 90° fire arc. Each tube takes configurable time to load (default 10 seconds).
- **Aft tube**: 1 tube.
- Torpedoes are always a manual attack — Weapons selects a tube and fires deliberately.
- Torpedoes display as a bright yellow sphere.
- Torpedoes are homing:
  - Limited, configurable turn rate (can be outmanoeuvred by a fast ship).
  - If the target is destroyed mid-flight, the torpedo continues straight and detonates on collision with anything it hits.
  - Torpedoes expire after a configurable timeout if they do not hit anything.
- Torpedo damage: configurable (default 50 vs. hull, 5 vs. shields).

## Targeting

- Weapons selects their own target by tapping on their radar display.
- The Science console's target is highlighted on the Weapons radar as a visual suggestion — advisory only, no mechanical coupling.

## Shields

- Four shield facings: fore, aft, port, starboard — tracked individually.
- Hit facing is determined by attacker bearing relative to the ship's current heading.
- Each facing has configurable HP (default 20).
- Shields regenerate naturally (configurable rate, default 1 HP per 3 seconds).
- If a shield facing is reduced to 0 HP it goes offline for a configurable duration (default 10 seconds). Full damage passes to hull while a facing is offline — no mitigation.
- Shield status (HP per facing) is shown on the Science console.

## Hull Damage & Repair

- Damage that passes through shields (or hits an offline facing) reduces hull integrity.
- Each console has a repair button that restores hull HP over time at `repair_rate × power_multiplier`.
- Only Engineering can see which consoles need repair. Individual console operators must be directed by Engineering.
- See Engineering Console doc for repair button details.

## Configurable Values (in ship entity config)

- Phaser damage, colour, cooldown, effective auto-fire range, arc reduction margin
- Torpedo count, damage, speed, turn rate, lifespan, load time, shield damage
- Shield HP per facing, regen rate, offline duration

All numbers should be configurable in the ship entity TOML.
