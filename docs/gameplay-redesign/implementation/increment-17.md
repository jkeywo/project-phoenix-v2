## Deception, access and emission intent

A spoofing emitter creates bounded supported signals/reports through the existing
canonical observation lifecycle, retaining provenance, original sample/receipt time
and known permissions. It never moves actors, changes colliders/physical damage or
upgrades stale claims into current truth. Correlation can preserve competing held
evidence; copies do not improve confidence. Apparent hulls do not gain a real body,
and no synthetic-only public flag may prove a decoy false. 19's physical decoy hull
can be detected/damaged independently of the misleading report it emits.

Bounded disruption uses a supported actual delivery path, target evidence, capability
and prerequisite/access policy, then applies only its declared information/effect
outcome with finite work/lifetime and normal counterplay. It cannot submit remote
ControlSystem commands or change enemy control source/AI. Hacking flavour does not
change transport security or grant privileged interior state. 16 captured-only
decoding/key policy remains its owner; simply waiting is not universal access.

Emission policy is local intent, not a truth/safety veto. Track actual supported
emitting operations and feed their causal signatures into existing passive/broad
acquisition. Cloak modifies supported detectability under its real resource/state
limits; firing/transmitting may increase exposure while cloak remains active unless
that specific profile is incompatible. Do not delete acquired tracks or prevent
ordinary weapons merely because a cloak/emission warning is visible. A human can
deliberately emit without an extra override ritual; AI uses the same admitted intent
and its actual known context. Preserve no-target/disabled intent on restored hardware.
