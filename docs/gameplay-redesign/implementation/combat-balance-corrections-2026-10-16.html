What remains unresolved

  • All 20 corrected runs reach 120 seconds still InProgress, with zero closing damage on both sides and no recorded System knockouts. The existing classifier therefore reports draws; no neutralization victory is recorded and the condition remains pending.
  • Forward tube launches rise from 35 to 81; torpedo-run manoeuvres return in 15 of 20 encounters. Fore-beam delivery improves against Destroyer, Cruiser and Patrol but falls against Warhawk.
  • Shadow now has a working authored standoff radius when enemy reach is unknown. Player shadow occupancy rises in three matchups; the defensive posture and re-engagement interaction needs investigation before assuming a damage-value increase is the next fix.
  • This matrix tests the combined changes, not one-fix-at-a-time causal attribution.

Changes

  • Helm treats a zero shield-gap requirement as optional, without inventing an observation.
  • Unknown enemy reach uses an explicit authored manoeuvre radius; observed reach retains precedence.
  • Single-shield Harrows opt out of focus in data. Mixed equipment ranks eligible focus separately from protection and recovery.
  • Cruiser fore beam uses its existing fixed arc; damage, range and cycle values are unchanged.
  • CLI duel victory uses the authored neutralization trigger, retaining actual death accounting.
