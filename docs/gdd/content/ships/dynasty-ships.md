The Dynasty player Cruiser is a distinct player-capable template composed from the Alliance Cruiser equipment baseline. It has its own faction, identity, model/theme, Station roster and authored strike reserve. It is not the Harrow NPC Cruiser and does not silently change that encounter hull.

## Current crew work

Six primary Stations divide the ship into Command, Helm, Gunnery, Sensors, Power and Damage Control. Their console pages, manuals and contextual tutorials describe the Dynasty layout while retaining the common admitted System commands, damage/repair, radar, weapons and Backfill substrates.

Gunnery adds a generation-backed strike reserve that can be committed for temporary firepower. Charging, enabled and depleted state make the extra decision visible. Exact generation, capacity, expenditure and output values belong in the composed hull and balance evidence. This is a current authored capability, not delivery of C4's whole component resource-network model or C5's loadout customisation.

## Current use and limits

The base Combat Test world offers the hull, and Cruiser Elimination fixes two Dynasty slots opposite two Alliance Cruisers. The curated demo still offers only Alliance Destroyer and Cruiser. The six-seat layout is a maximum division of primary work, not a ratified human crew recommendation.

**Plan — T5:** close larger-session integrated journeys and real-network/long-session/accessibility/language acceptance. Hull-specific balance reports are evidence about tested matchups, not a completed fairness or station-engagement claim for every scenario.
