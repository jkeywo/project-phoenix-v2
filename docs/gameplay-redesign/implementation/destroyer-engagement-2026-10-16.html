Why the fights stalled

The original seed 1 trace was still in active impulse with a current target 30.32° off-bow, lost the contact, then continued at speed 84; separation reached3551 units by 55 seconds. A torpedo-hold-only experiment still ran away. The runtime correction keeps the same trace within 293 units over 120 seconds and repeatedly closes back into range.

That correction alone did not solve everything: in two extended runs, the Cruiser stood down and stopped adding damage. The selected30-second combat window keeps all five extended runs dealing player damage at their final checkpoint.

A tested300-unit Captain trigger was rejected. Review found the Captain's existing proximity facts read the physical world snapshot without a radar filter. Increasing that trigger would expand an existing knowledge leak. The selected timer change uses actual combat timestamps and leaves that trigger unchanged. No 180-unit defensive-ring mismatch is claimed.
