What the timing now measures

neutralization_group = "side_b" explicitly binds the duel's enemy group. The metric reads the recorded firing time of its unique once-only, ungated, zero-delay on_all_neutralized trigger in a victory/GameOver report. It does not use the run-end timestamp, an earlier escort death or a bare victory label.

Non-victories are excluded. Missing, ambiguous or invalid evidence is counted; one missing victorious sample makes the timing gate unmeasurable, rather than improving the median. Literal death-based TTK remains available separately. This contract is deliberately limited to explicitly bound canonical duels.

The numerical timing limits remain unchanged: destroyer ≤ 10s; cruiser ≤ 25s; patrol ≤ 20s; warhawk ≤ 25s. Wins alone do not establish that the timing gates pass.
