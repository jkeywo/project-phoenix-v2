## Outcome and selected scope

Make a bounded active sector's facilities, workers, services, shipments and actors
causally affect one another through actual bridge operations. A failed facility
component or intercepted delivery changes what can be done, rather than merely
selecting an unrelated scenario penalty. Keep causes and uncertainty visible
through permitted observations/reports. The catalogue and
[PASM contract](../../../pasm/spec/design/facilities-actors.yaml) govern.

## Physical service and dependency contract

composed Dock System. A configured producer must have its owning Console,
physical placement and Operation component, and a local energy consumer with
positive continuous work cost. Axiom and its existing manual-trade provider
bind their actual Dock; an omitted binding retains legacy replenishment and is
not evidence that every facility has common equipment.

Each next work step stages the visitor Umbilical and provider Dock energy plus
the actual finite stock/quota/capacity exchange in stable visitor UUID order.
Preview clones are transaction-local only. Neither endpoint pays a rejected
final plan. The existing PortOrder fraction and actual receipt remain the sole
work frontier; there is no second facility progress timer, queue or crew pool.
Zero Operation, explicit Offline or unavailable funding pauses real work;
partial Operation/funding bounds delivered work. A delivered receipt exists
only after actual stock commits. Interruption feedback identifies the known
coupled service's availability without disclosing foreign reserve values or
private component detail.

Preserve actual stock quantities/ceilings, terms/quotas, rigs and physical
conditions. Axiom already has common Dock/refit machinery, so no competing
coarse condition needs removal there. The transfer depot's independent scenario
condition and workforce/disposition register remain unchanged. This consumer
does not deliver shipments/dependency networks, physical workforce, outside
actors, full21 acceptance or unfinished20 Medical/Security/external work.

Author each service's actual producer System/equipment, compatible consumed inputs,
outputs/stores, required workers/access and supported interruption behaviour.
Energy/Stock/teams remain their actual owners. The next indivisible work step claims
its required set together, pays only actual granted work and records the real result;
no broad reservation for every possible future service stage. Already loaded or
in-progress physical goods remain real on interruption. Recovery never silently
refills stock, repairs components or clears receipts/obligations.

Declare dependency edges to concrete supported service/resource outputs. Evaluate
stable causal changes through the existing event/effect/settlement pipeline, not
mirrored scenario counters or recursive same-tick free production. Validate cycles:
reject unsupported instantaneous recursion, while a real storage/delay boundary may
support a feedback network under an explicit execution rule. No authorable physical
processor schedule. Thresholds derive from actual capacity/availability, publish
causes and dated reports, and emit each real crossing once. Restoration/removal must
rebuild derived service access before the first new command.

Physical effects may consult actual equipment and stock to resolve operation;
remote knowledge requires normal reports/observations/Comms 16. A sender cannot
read a facility's hidden current stores or mark delivery complete from a sent
request. Friendly access/security/terms remain explicit. Common shields/Operation
conditions degrade real capability; repair/refit uses actual teams/parts/energy and
facility/provider status, without a special invulnerable starbase service path.

Workforce tasks bind actual 20 team identities and locations. Worker availability,
competency, injury, disposition and route affect real work; strike/consent is not
teleporting/despawning a population or corrupting equipment state. Negotiate and
change practical conditions through supported outcomes/known information. Anonymous
workers may aggregate but conservation, custody and finite concurrent commitments
must match the shared team model. Foundations can prepare actual machinery/stock
without pretending this later workforce join is already delivered.

## Traffic, shipments and bounded autonomous actors

An active-sector shipment has one actual vessel/cargo owner and departure/arrival
frontier. Stock leaves its real source on committed dispatch/loading and arrives
only through actual receipt/unloading into compatible space. Reuse current cargo/
Stock, logistics, ports and craft custody interfaces, with no parallel transit
inventory or timer that credits goods regardless of physical arrival. Loss, diverted
routes, full receiving stores or unavailable service retains supported actual
cargo/location/outcome and releases claims according to the real job phase.
Off-sector abstraction must not silently replace an in-sector physical leg.

Routine transport uses ordinary AI and actual 18 motion/navigation. Existing traffic
vessels suffice where compatible; 19 is required for a newly carried/deployed craft,
not all delivery activity. 16 carries remote orders/receipts; 20 carries workforce/
personnel, never cargo-count mirrors of people. Each receiving actor rechecks its
own objectives, authority, knowledge, willingness and actual capacity. Emergencies
may change supported priority or permission, not grant a controller hidden truth or
universal override of another human's station.

Bound the number of active actors/goals and use authored/systemic triggers for
needs, rather than simulate an entire galaxy. Existing per-System AI executes each
approved local action under normal admission. Actors can supply, repair, secure,
evacuate or otherwise satisfy supported work using actual services/stock/teams.
Their availability is a fact to discover, not a scripted automatic win/lose clock.
Public chatter/reports carry attributed intent, acceptance and outcomes separately.

Use existing independent Objective instances, membership and fixed completion-credit
contracts. Actual competing work can change a need; it cannot retroactively rewrite
who completed an already credited objective or grant a benefit twice. Authors can
protect meaningful crew opportunities explicitly; do not invent failure or fresh
chores merely because an ally successfully helped. A known commitment/deadline is
not silently retuned by outside activity. 22–24 admission/pacing later uses these
actual outcomes without owning their machinery.

Relationships combine faction context with actor-known interactions/commitments;
information spreads only through supported communications/evidence. Generic requests
do not create universal formal obligations, automatic risk judgements or omniscient
reputation. Surrender changes intent with observable conduct, not magical capture;
whole-ship operational capture uses 20. Coarse facility/territory control reflects
real work and declared authority; no new colony-resource budget or RTS ownership
panel is implied. Particular new construction/development mechanics remain unselected
until an actual supported consumer is specified.

## Explicit transfer and the first physical supply leg

The existing Umbilical AI currently enables free-port Main replenishment before
checking a standing Transfer directive. That opportunistic mode always collects
Main deficits and can replace intended unloading. Preserve idle unattended
replenishment, including the shipped default Deliver configuration; the configured
direction alone is not an explicit donation order.

A permitted standing Transfer takes precedence: ordinary admitted controls disable
port maintenance and start the selected resource/direction/store. With no such
order, the individual AI enables opportunistic replenishment only while idle.
Already active human work keeps its chosen mode during delegation. Existing
standing-operation ownership still controls withdrawal and never stops an
unclaimed human flow. This changes no inventory, movement or service owner.

The bounded physical consumer loads finite goods at one actual equipment-backed
facility, carries them on an ordinary vessel through actual undock/travel/dock,
and unloads through the second actual provider's paid finite service. Crew or
normal authored goals may select the two legs; their fulfillment requires real
movement and receipts. No shipment progress timer, mirrored cargo, new route AI,
foreign stock query or automatic scenario-completion flag is added. Validate the
actual source/vessel/receiver conservation, unavailable/full service pauses and
complete fresh-host JSON continuation. This foundation does not finish 21's
outside-actor goal selection, workforce or whole-increment acceptance.

Transfer also joins the existing Navigation objective-to-waypoint arm used by
Dock. Resolve its named approach through the identical permitted contact/request
path and ordinary waypoint/Helm-clearance mechanism. This fills the existing
Transfer chain's approach consumer; it adds no route controller, private position,
new observation grant or remote authority. Unknown/obfuscated targets retain the
same knowledge boundary. Crew-chosen waypoints remain an ordinary alternative.
