This record does not claim a complete playable exit or final repository gates.

## Current implementation

Equipped entities use one Gameplay ShipDamage owner for compartment structure,
component conditions and causal dose. Planar material penetration, weighted decks
and retained beam samples replace percentage System damage. Existing actual hit,
collision, hazard and supported GM producers use this owner. Independent operation,
intake and cooling effects act on the real machinery, energy and protection owners.
Legacy unequipped props retain their separate HP model.

Existing repair jobs retain physical routes, exact structural/component stages,
finite source-parts reservations and paid/manual work. External teams restore
visited physical actors without inventing target-side crews. Local and foreign
teams share exact task exclusion through their existing jobs. Pending orders wait;
recall, range loss and disappeared targets release only unused real claims.

Console-filtered status/details, interior controls, repair reports and work lists
show the same permitted physical state. Fresh on-site diagnoses supersede older
same-System evidence; equal-tick reports retain the worst reading. GM Live exposes
checked effects while damage/layout/impact authoring requires recreation.

Recoverable neutralization preserves entities and crew seats. Its public coarse
indication travels only with normally visible contacts. Reversible event frontiers,
current UUID membership and authored combat conditions distinguish defeat from
literal destruction. Deliberate destruction and unrelated scenario chains remain.

The compatibility boundary is protocol 8, base content epoch 3, snapshot 46 and
rules 0.18. Shipped hull data, generated equipment and deterministic pack fixtures
are migrated. Restore preflight validates owners, complete target shapes, finite
source reservations and cross-actor task claims before live writes.
