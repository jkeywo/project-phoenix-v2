# Star Trek Online — duty-officer follow-up

Reviewed 2026-10-04 for G068/G069 and S079/S080. This supplements the saved R2 research and the earlier partially accessible story-assignment reference.

## Verified primary-source scope

Senior systems designer Geoff Tuffli's [2012 developer guest blog](https://www.startrek.com/en-un/news/guest-blog-sto-reinforcements-duty-officer-pack) describes officer specialisations, traits and active-roster abilities. Examples include carrier-mode-dependent bonuses, weapon recharge effects and crew recovery. It also describes trade-assignment contacts and prisoner/contraband assignment access. These establish historical personnel/assignment variety; they do not establish current rules, staffing requirements, durations or success probabilities.

## Proposed Phoenix transfers

- Named specialists could make assignment choices more meaningful than interchangeable team counts.
- Keep basic typed-team work viable without a particular named officer; expertise could improve a supported method or outcome.
- Assignment opportunity cost should concern the current ship/scenario: the same expertise cannot be everywhere at once.
- Low-key external assignments should return useful evidence or operational effects rather than become reward-collection timers.
- Avoid copying collectible rarity ladders, pack purchases, MMO-scale rosters or combat-stat stacking as design requirements.

These are proposals. G068/G069 and later System review decide inclusion and exact form. The source does not demonstrate enjoyable six-player bridge activity or verify the proposed team framework.
