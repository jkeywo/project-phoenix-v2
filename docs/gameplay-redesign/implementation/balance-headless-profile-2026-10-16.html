The old torpedo_run phase disappears from all current duels. No player torpedoes launch against Destroyers or Warhawks. Some launch against Cruisers and Patrols, but their contribution falls.

The leading explanation is a coupled combat interaction.

In the unchanged 30-second Destroyer trace, four nominal six-second beams last only 0.90, 2.02, 0.15 and 1.65 seconds. Normal cooldown follows. The sampled banks retain 24–30/30 energy, negligible heat and released restraint.

Tracking or range cuts firing windows→Cooldown reduces sustained damage→Shields recover; torpedo opportunities shrink→Long lulls and defensive movement sustain the fight

This chain is a working explanation, not a fully isolated root cause. Source policy gates aggressive movement on Red Alert; alert loss sends Helm to shadow. No free shield recharge, runaway repair, exact physical-miss rate or isolated Power defect has been established.

Respect the observation frontier. Weapon readiness matches the preceding permitted radar publication, not necessarily the radar refreshed in the same outgoing message. At the four cutoffs, aim discrepancies are inside their published uncertainty; physical samples also lag 17–33 ms. The trace does not report authoritative beam-end reasons.

Longer engagements could help crew coordination. These results do not yet demonstrate that benefit: curtailed firing and stronger survival are producing stalemates. Restoring the old four-second kill time is not assumed to be the redesign’s goal.

Method and validity

Three control → wrapped → control sequences per world, rotating world order. Seed 42, six-seat Cruiser, 60 simulated seconds. First 300 App updates excluded; 3301 retained per run. Conventional median across six controls; median across three wrapped runs. All 18 captures exit successfully, retain untruncated traces and match tick 3600 plus the earlier production digest. Sampled background CPU is 0.164–0.267 cores; no competing compiler is observed.

The stripped measure build uses named Bevy system wrappers. This is not a PDB/stack-sampling profile, a clean-release gate, rendering/native-display timing or Human workload evidence. Runtime/content hashes remain unchanged; unrelated untracked output is preserved. Raw process samples, source/binary hashes and complete traces are retained locally.
