This catalogue collects existing Phoenix capabilities, PASM Plan and the recovered research. It supplies ingredients for the [new GDD](./README.md), without selecting a six-Station roster or making every system mandatory. [Game systems](./game-systems.md) describes the surrounding rules, actors and scenario pressures.

## Status and activity notation

**Current** identifies the reviewed implementation, not universal availability on every hull or completed human acceptance. **Plan Cn** and **Plan unscheduled** refer to the existing roadmap. **Research** identifies a mechanism in a source, without claiming Phoenix implements or has committed to it. **Proposal** identifies an exploratory extension from this brief or catalogue analysis. Mixed labels state which part exists and which part does not.

**O** means ongoing activity or monitoring whenever the relevant machinery or situation is operating; **E** means an event-driven intervention. These are the user's passive/active distinction: passive work can still involve frequent decisions. A weapon need not fire during peaceful travel to have an ongoing operating loop during combat. Activity and delegation columns are **candidate design analysis**, including for Current rows; they do not claim that every suggested AI adapter already exists.

An AI assistant takes over an entire named System. It can execute a bounded intent such as firing approved targets or towing an approved mission subject. Human responsibility can remain at target selection, risk limits, priorities and consequential choices. The actor who gives permission and the System that carries it out must remain clear.

## Weapons and ordnance

**Selected common model:** weapon capabilities below are equipment/role configurations built from a generic weapon System and reusable behaviours. Configure arc, traversal/tracking speed, effective range, targeting rules, projectile type/speed, damage amount/type, area of effect and barrel selections/combinations, with compatible resource/loading/guidance behaviours. **Authors define modes with a barrel-marker list and an arbitrary-length ordered cycle; each step names one or more barrels. Cycles fire ready selected barrels and advance past unavailable ones.** Players select modes rather than constructing patterns during play. Support alternating twin guns, missile racks and coordinated multi-barrel/Itano Circus-style launch patterns, with flight behaviour composed separately. Preserve individual AI per configured System and existing communication/knowledge/authority rules. These are proposal capabilities, not Current implementation claims. Exact valid combinations and timing/reset remain open.

## Catalogue boundaries and next selection

Hull shape, mass, mount legality, fitting budgets and faction doctrine affect these capabilities but are **game rules**, catalogued separately. Controllers, phones, network protocols and rendering backends are delivery facilities, not extra player jobs. First-person actions, landing combat and unrestricted ship construction stay visible as research boundaries rather than becoming implied requirements.

For each shortlisted capability, write an activity card specifying its observed state, recurring decision, event intervention, information owner, external requests, reserve/grace period, whole-System AI intent, failure/recovery and novice/mastery paths. Then test bundles against six humans and the 2–8 support range. Do not infer Station count from this catalogue's length.
