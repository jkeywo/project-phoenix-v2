## Reading historical research

Read R1 and R2 as inventories of mechanisms and design questions. Use the [current GDD](../../gdd/foundation/current-baseline.md) for what Phoenix does today and the [PASM roadmap](../../../pasm/spec/roadmap/phoenix-delivery-roadmap.yaml) for existing Plan. Research inclusion neither revives an obsolete scope constraint nor ratifies a feature.

The catalogues cite R1's numbered entries and R2's named sections where possible. The activity, decision and AI notes beside those entries are **proposal analysis**, not claims that a competitor uses exactly that player loop. New ideas without evidence are marked **Proposal**.
