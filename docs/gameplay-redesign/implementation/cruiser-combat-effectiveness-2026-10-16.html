Why fights stalled

The engagement fixes restored attack passes, but a nominal 32-DPS beam still did not guarantee 32 structural damage per second. The physical damage path distributes energy using material resistance, path thickness and penetration. A high penetration budget can pass through a lightly built Destroyer without depositing most of its payload.

The Destroyer has two 10-unit compartments: light bridge material (laser resistance 0.15) and bulkhead engineering material (0.7). With penetration 10, an intact straight 10-unit path deposits 15% in the bridge alone or 70% in engineering alone. Damage and oblique paths change those fractions. These are formula examples, not measured average hit fractions.

Range also matters against a faster opponent. Moving reach from 40 to 55 extends possible firing exposure during a pass. The ablations below establish the combination's benefit; exact cutoff and ray-miss contributions were not instrumented. Enemy repair was zero in the prior long diagnostics, so repair was not undoing all progress.

Selected authored change

  • Standard fixed fore and aft beams: range 40 → 55, penetration 10 → 2.
  • Mirror both values in the default fitted fore-beam assembly.
  • Keep 32 DPS, six-second burn/rest and fixed arcs. Keep torpedoes, shields, enemy durability and optional pulse fitting coefficients.

The existing damage mechanism deposits more of the same nominal payload locally. This is a real effective-damage buff, despite unchanged nominal DPS. No simulation code, networking or optimisation work changed.
