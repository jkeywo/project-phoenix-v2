AD01 · Selected direction

A Console owns control bindings; a Station hosts Consoles

Each operable System has one controlling Console. Fixed or human-seeking placement assigns that Console to a Station. Several Console screens can appear as tabs for one player. A read-only display of another System does not acquire its control authority.

AD37 · User direction A physical compartment and a controlling Console

Each System lives physically in a compartment and is controlled through a Console. These are separate relationships to the same System. One compartment can contain Systems controlled by several Consoles; one Console can control Systems in several compartments.

Coarse compartment hazards and safety margins retain S029/S102's selected rules; physical hit paths, repair access and conduit routes use the ship layout. Compartment membership does not grant knowledge or command authority. Current damage/repair implementation remains System/Station-oriented and needs migration to this proposed composition.

AD38 · Selected A initially A System's equipment stays in its compartment

Equipment in another compartment is a separate System connected through explicit services/data. Each System retains its own controlling Console, shared command channels and individual AI. Compartment templates contain complete fitted Systems.

A central pump can serve equipment in other compartments. Physical conduits retain their authored routes and damage locations; those routes do not relocate the connected equipment.

Exact boundary/exterior equipment and conduit-segment ownership need their own layout contract. This selection must not erase coarse damageable routes or require an operator for every segment.

AD39 · Selected B, extended by AD45 Planar penetration within a selected deck

Ships can have multiple decks, each with a 2D compartment layout. Incoming damage selects a weighted random deck independently of shot Z height, then penetrates along its incoming planar direction. Compartments own material properties and HP; their damage applies component status effects.

A shot aimed toward engines from the front encounters the compartments along that approach first. Target knowledge helps planar aim but does not guarantee the engine's deck is selected. Under AD46, losing compartment HP progressively reduces its resistance to later shots.

Small craft and torpedoes retain their selected movement and presentation. Hit handling supplies the planar direction from which damage arrives; it must not silently replace that direction with the firing ship's bearing after a projectile turns. Exact conversion for blast sources or near-vertical approaches remains an integration detail.

Existing planar arc geometry provides bearing conventions. Current ship damage uses weighted HP entries, and repair teams use configured travel duration. These need mapping to the proposed internal layout and access rules.

Exact 2D shapes, payload deposition and the resistance curve remain open. Teams follow supported passages and connections between decks; a damage path does not automatically become a walkable route. Scanning and knowledge limits, capital-ship movement and coarse atmosphere retain their selected rules.

AD40 · Selected A Distributed penetration lines for ordinary hits

Ordinary hits enter along distributed lines across the facing profile, governed by supported weapon accuracy/spread rules. Each follows the incoming planar direction through intervening compartments. Deliberate subsystem targeting concentrates aim toward the compartment known to contain that equipment, subject to accuracy, protection and weighted deck selection. Components have no finer position to target.

Ordinary fire can affect different compartments across the facing area. Focused targeting and a sustained approach angle can concentrate damage, subject to weighted deck selection. Progressive breakthrough uses compartment state; normal hits do not always follow the centre line.

This choice neither restores 3D impact-point damage nor adds a second hit/miss roll. Exact accuracy integration, sampling and repeatability remain to be specified, with the same rules for human and AI attackers.

AD41 · Selected A Separate penetration and payload parameters

Configure penetration strength/profile independently from delivered damage and supported effects. Common material interactions determine continuation and deposition along the planar path.

A shot can penetrate deeply while doing modest local damage, or deliver heavy damage with little penetration. These are data-driven variations within the same supported weapon framework.

Independent parameters do not apply the full payload to every traversed compartment or affected component. Attenuation, deposition, stopping and residual penetration still need rules that prevent repeated damage creation. AD44 locates structural HP in compartments and applies component status effects from their damage.

Existing shield-pierce routing and torpedo payload fields are migration inputs. Shield bypass is distinct from penetrating armour or internal material; a penetration parameter alone supplies neither bypass nor hidden targeting knowledge.

AD42 · User direction Material properties belong to the compartment

Set material properties once per compartment, as part of its definition/template. There are no separate material assignments or overrides for individual Systems, parts, bulkheads or map cells. Component and System templates use the material context of the compartment where they are placed.

Components retain individual faults and repair targets. Sharing a compartment does not make every impact produce identical faults. Zero HP does apply the common compartment-sourced disabling condition under AD49, with recovery under AD50.

Workshop exposes material and structural HP configuration at compartment scope. The runtime retains current compartment HP separately from component status effects. Exact material fields, boundary attribution and damage-to-condition rules remain open.

AD43 · Selected A Resistance scales with path length

The compartment's effective material resistance is charged per unit of 2D distance. A longer traversal spends more penetration. Approach angle and depth matter, without finer material assignments or an arbitrary extra cost each time an otherwise unchanged region is divided into compartments.

AD46 makes effective resistance progressively fall as compartment HP falls. Exact stopping, payload deposition, damage-type response and the resistance curve still need rules.

AD44 · User direction Compartments have HP; components have status effects

State ownerDamage and consequences
CompartmentOwns structural HP and material properties. Deposited damage reduces its HP; the path length determines resistance cost.
Component / physical partReceives status effects from compartment damage. Conditions affect its supported mechanisms and remain individual repair targets; no component HP pool.

AD48 selects effects from the compartment-wide eligible component pool; exact types, weights and severity remain open. At zero HP, AD49 disables the contents through a condition removed by compartment restoration. Individual component faults remain separate. Progressive breaches use compartment state, without a local hole grid or individual wall HP.

AD45 · User direction Weighted random deck, independent of shot height

Author ships with multiple decks, each containing a 2D compartment layout. Incoming damage selects a weighted random deck before tracing its planar path. The shot's Z height does not select or override the deck.

Physical composition is ship → decks → compartments → fitted Systems/components. Console control remains a separate relationship. Workshop needs deck-aware placement and visible selection weights; repair teams use explicit connections between decks with automatic routing.

Weight sources/defaults, eligibility for different deck footprints and repeatable sampling remain open. Known component targeting does not guarantee its deck. This selection adds neither deck HP nor a 3D internal damage trajectory.

AD46 · Selected A Compartment damage progressively weakens resistance

Effective resistance falls as compartment HP falls. Repeated damage makes the compartment progressively easier for later shots to penetrate. Material properties, current HP and path length supply the resistance calculation; the exact curve remains tunable.

Different paths through the same damaged compartment share its current resistance. Gradual breakthrough uses compartment state, without adding local holes, weakened cells or component HP.

Component effects, structural repair and ship defeat keep their separate rules. Exact residual resistance at zero HP and whether a hit's own damage affects its remaining traversal still need specification.

AD47 · Selected A Damage events generate component status effects

Individual damaging events can apply component effects. Authored rules use deposited damage amount/type and component susceptibility to determine eligible effects, chance and severity. Small hits need not cause a fault; later damage can cause new faults or affect existing ones under supported condition rules.

Compartment HP controls structural resistance; damage events generate component faults. Repairing an impact fault does not have it continuously reapplied merely because HP remains low. AD49's compartment-sourced disabling condition is separate and needs structural restoration to clear.

Reuse the common condition mechanism. EMP refresh/resistance and escalation retain their own rules. Effect counts, severity and probability curves remain open; continuous beams, rapid fire and tick splitting need consistent event accounting so update frequency does not accidentally determine fault rates.

AD48 · Selected B, clarified Components belong to a compartment without precise positions

Components have compartment membership, identity, conditions and susceptibility, but no internal coordinates or geometric footprint. Damage selects effects across the compartment's eligible components using damage type and susceptibility. There is no proximity-to-path weighting inside the compartment.

Scans identify the compartment containing equipment. Firing-angle advice aims toward that region through intervening protection; it cannot pick a particular component from its occupants or guarantee the right deck.

Workshop assigns Systems/components to compartment contents. Component icons can be arranged for readability without physical meaning. Repair teams route to the compartment and work on an identified component; there is no finer location to navigate to. Conduit routes and external weapon/sensor geometry retain their separate contracts.

S025 already permits full field restoration of structural protection with suitable materials, teams, access and time, subject to G064's catastrophic exceptions. Carry that direction into compartment HP repair separately from component-condition repair; exact recipes and work limits remain open.

AD49 · User direction Compartment restoration removes a disabling condition

At zero compartment HP, a condition disables its contained components. Restoring the compartment removes that condition. Components retain their individual identity and other conditions throughout.

Remove only the condition sourced by the compartment. A repaired compartment can still contain damaged, EMP-disabled or destroyed equipment; repairing an individual component cannot clear the compartment's disabling condition.

Reuse the common condition and repair mechanisms. Status details identify the compartment cause through permitted knowledge; structural work uses the agreed materials, teams and access rules. Clearing the condition permits operation only when other conditions, supply, current commands and startup rules allow it.

AD50 selects a modest authored recovery threshold. Residual resistance, safe access, continuing damage at zero HP and whole-ship defeat have separate rules; disablement grants no immunity and causes no automatic physical deletion.

AD50 · Selected A Recover above zero, before full structural restoration

Disable at zero compartment HP; remove the condition when repairs reach an authored HP fraction above zero and below full restoration. Further work can continue restoring protection. The exact fraction remains tunable.

Once applied, the condition persists until the recovery threshold is reached. Once cleared, it reapplies only at zero HP—not merely because HP falls below the recovery threshold again.

Other component faults remain. Clearing this condition permits operation under current commands, supply and startup requirements; it does not introduce a manual restart step. The shared report list and independent-job framework already support structural and component repairs as distinct targets.

AD51 · Selected A Structural and component repairs can proceed in parallel

Teams can repair components while another restores the compartment, provided access, safety, materials, tools and other real job requirements permit. The compartment's disabling condition alone does not block repair work.

A repaired component remains disabled until the compartment reaches its recovery threshold. Its completion report must retain that known limitation; a structural recovery report does not claim that other component faults were repaired.

Use independent jobs with distinct targets, progress and resources in the shared report-driven Repair workload. Keep one team per component, automatic routing and ordinary Repair control/AI. Structural-team stacking and exact rates remain separate; this choice grants neither automatic dispatch nor hidden knowledge.

AD52 · Selected B, clarified Combat can end before physical destruction

Compartment HP depletion alone leaves a disabled vessel. Physical destruction requires a supported catastrophe, such as a reactor explosion or completed self-destruct procedure. Crew may abandon ship earlier, and losing engines and power can establish disablement.

State or assessmentMeaning
Physically destroyedA supported catastrophic cause produces actual ship loss. No aggregate compartment-HP destruction threshold.
DisabledLoss of usable propulsion and power; the vessel remains present and potentially recoverable.
AbandonedCrew evacuation, possibly before disablement. Physical personnel state is separate from a player leaving a seat.
Assessed out of actionA human or individual System AI has sufficient permitted evidence to treat the ship as disabled or abandoned for its objective. Evidence and uncertainty remain visible.

AI needs a clear operational endpoint without waiting for an explosion. Use observed capability loss, evacuation and Comms through existing Console knowledge processing; do not expose a hidden global death flag.

Existing objective/doctrine rules govern each AI's reaction, including ceasing engagement, reprioritising or supporting recovery. Continued hostile activity and later observed recovery remain relevant. A status label does not switch off real weapons, grant immunity or automatically resolve every mission.

Capability roles are authored: an intentionally engineless turret has not lost propulsion, and reactor failure does not prove loss of power while usable batteries remain. Exact capability, confidence and persistence criteria remain open. Preserve the engines-and-power criterion for ordinary propelled ships.

Current SystemHull destruction requires every System HP entry to reach zero. The proposal replaces that physical-destruction rule and distinguishes it from targeting assessments, objectives and entity lifetime. Early evacuation uses ordinary personnel/craft/work mechanisms; AD54 selects initiation authority, while exact escape consequences remain to be specified.

AD53 · Selected A Abandonment orders non-hostile standby

The normal abandonment procedure issues cease-fire/standby orders, retaining supported evacuation, life support and distress functions where possible. Ordinary command channels and individual System AI execute authorised operations within physical constraints and transition timing. Human-held Systems retain their normal control authority.

This creates a legible end to fighting and an opportunity for rescue through existing command/procedure mechanisms. It needs no special Backfill behaviour or central controller.

Standby does not instantly shut down machinery, erase damage or already-fired projectiles, trigger self-destruct, force surrender or guarantee safety. Other actors still assess evidence and follow their objectives/doctrine. AD54 selects initiation authority; authored exceptions remain to be defined.

AD54 · Selected A Humans initiate abandonment when aboard

AI can report danger and recommend evacuation, but a human starts abandonment when human players are aboard. Ordinary individual AI executes authorised steps for delegated Systems; human-held Systems retain their control authority. Fully AI-crewed ships may initiate through ordinary System AI policies using permitted knowledge and objectives.

The controlling System/Console remains data-driven. No vote, mandatory captain approval, central controller or special Backfill behaviour is added.

AD53 supplies the normal standby orders. Actual evacuation uses available personnel, craft and work mechanisms. Exact AI triggers, evacuation steps and cancellation rules remain to be specified. Evacuation is distinct from a player leaving a seat or disconnecting.

AD55 · Selected A Return an abandoned ship to service in the field

Authorised crew can return through supported personnel transfer, restore needed capabilities and resume operating the ship during the same scenario. Reuse ordinary ship, team, repair and Console mechanisms; abandonment alone requires no port recovery.

Preserve the existing entity, Systems and Consoles, including damage, conditions, supplies, ongoing physical work and permitted retained knowledge. Returning crew does not reset the vessel.

Normal authority, individual System AI, resource and startup requirements govern resumed operation. Particular repairs can still need facilities. Reboarding does not revive irreparable equipment, automatically resume previous hostile orders or clear other observers' assessments. This concerns a ship the returning crew is authorised to operate; hostile capture and exact access requirements remain separate.

AD56 · Selected A initially Capture transfers whole-ship operational authority

Meeting supported authored capture requirements transfers authority for the vessel as a whole. Before that handover, located teams can disable equipment, secure areas and pursue other supported objectives. Occupying one System does not grant partial command authority.

Keep the same ship, physical state, System/Console topology and individual controllers. A ship-wide authority transfer introduces no central ship AI and changes no transport architecture.

Former operators lose authority based on their former tenure; admission, reassignment and publication must reflect the handover coherently. Behavioural surrender alone does not transfer control. Exact capture requirements, remaining defenders, post-capture orders and useful participation for displaced players still need contracts. Capture neither repairs the vessel nor grants hidden world knowledge.

AD57 · Selected A Captors inherit readable local Console records

Newly authorised operators can read retained sensor observations, decoded Comms and reports without a universal extraction job. Preserve source, age, receipt history where relevant and uncertainty. Capture neither reconstructs lost records nor makes historical observations current.

Records stay scoped to their Consoles. Human operators and individual System AIs receive the same permitted knowledge; normal AI reading time still applies. Ordinary sharing retains evidence lineage.

Other ships' private knowledge, unread encrypted material and private AI memory do not become readable Console facts. Ongoing feeds still obey faction, sharing, communication and authorisation rules; capture grants no automatic continuing access to the former faction's live intelligence or automatic merging into friendly Consoles. Retention and exact access/revocation contracts remain to be specified.

AD58 · Selected A Shared compartment connections for teams and deliveries

Compartments form route nodes joined by authored passages and inter-deck connections. Teams and internal deliveries automatically choose permitted routes without calculating detailed paths inside rooms. Workshop edits these connections beside the compartment layouts; players give destinations, work and priorities.

Reuse topology and route queries, retaining each activity's access, safety, travel and capacity rules. Ordinary System controllers choose work; independent jobs execute it. The route mechanism is not another AI controller.

A movement passage does not automatically carry power, coolant or atmosphere. Each mechanism uses its appropriate authored relationships. Damage still penetrates compartment geometry independently of movement routes. Specific passage equipment, travel costs, hazards and interruption rules remain to be defined.

AD59 · Selected A Aggregate batches account for supplies in transit

A delivery batch holds a resource quantity, source, destination and route stage/progress. Dispatch removes its quantity from source stock; arrival deposits it at the destination. Reuse shared resources, independent jobs and compartment routing. Ordinary logistics AI handles routine replenishment.

No rendered cargo actor, individual item or porter is required. Reports preserve normal knowledge limits; an unseen loss does not become known merely because authoritative stock changed. Actual access, safety and capacity still apply. AD61 selects general handling capacity. AD60 · Selected A Dispatch reserves compatible receiving space

Before a batch starts travelling, reserve its compatible destination capacity. Other ordinary loading and deliveries respect that claim. Undispatched requests reserve no destination space, reusing stage-start commitments rather than locking the whole future queue.

Tie the claim to its delivery and destination. Dispatch must establish it alongside the source-to-transit stock movement; refusal leaves no departed batch or dangling claim. Arrival turns reserved capacity into actual occupancy.

Damage, isolation or changed compatibility can still prevent receipt. The batch retains its actual in-transit state; diversion, cancellation and loss reconcile claims without teleporting or restoring cargo. Ordinary logistics AI handles routine execution and reports exceptions. Exact arbitration, invalidation and recovery contracts remain open.

AD61 · Selected A Routine deliveries use general handling capacity

The logistics System supplies authored throughput and concurrent-work limits, subject to its fitted requirements, resources, conditions and routes. Shared capacity and job mechanisms bound deliveries; ordinary logistics AI schedules routine replenishment using its priorities and permitted knowledge.

No separately assigned personnel team is required for each batch. Keep compartment travel, actual in-transit stock and receiving-space claims. A change in handling capacity does not erase or relocate cargo already travelling.

Shortages, delays and blockages use ordinary job/report feedback. Repair/security teams and the shared framework for any explicitly modelled workforce remain unchanged. Exact rates, concurrency accounting, equipment costs, interruption policy and specialist transport work remain to be defined.

AD65 · Selected A Request resources, resolve availability, then consume

Components declare requirements before consuming resources. Shared accounting at the relevant store or network resolves usable amounts against actual supply, capacity, reservations and selected allocation rules. Two weapons sharing a battery cannot spend the same energy; resource policy determines precedence rather than incidental processor order.

A · Request, resolve, then apply

Components declare resource requirements. Accounting at the relevant store/network resolves usable amounts under actual limits and selected allocation rules. Operations consume only their granted resources.

B · Consume in fixed order

Each operation checks and withdraws stock in its engine-defined turn. Later operations use what remains; deterministic processor order determines precedence.

Selected: A. Requests, grants and actual consumption are distinct. Operations use only granted resources and return explicit fulfilled/unmet results. This is shared accounting, not another AI controller or a universal solver for every resource.

Power rationing and flow cuts, local batteries and heat, recipe output limits and job reservations retain their selected rules. Exact arbitration, same-tick production visibility, grant lifetime and commit/revalidation remain open. Admitted intent and physical results remain distinct from permitted Console knowledge.

AD66 · Selected A Secure each work step's required resources together

The next indivisible work step obtains its required resources and capacities together. A failed attempt retains no new partial claims or consumption. For example, a fabrication step lacking energy does not newly reserve its available parts while waiting. AD23 still limits commitments to the next executable stage.

A · Commit the required set together

Secure the next indivisible step's required resources and capacities together. A failed attempt keeps no new partial claims. Explicit preparation and delivery stages can make their own valid commitments.

B · Hold partial claims

Reserve available inputs while waiting for missing requirements, until they arrive or the controller cancels/releases the claims. Blocked work can therefore withhold resources from other work.

Selected: A. Explicit preparation and delivery stages can make their own valid commitments. Loaded ammunition, charged batteries, delivered supplies and existing valid reservations persist. Continuous flows and passive demands keep their own rules. Exact arbitration, revalidation and claim lifetime remain execution contracts.

AD67 · Selected A Priority changes what starts next

Changing priority reorders waiting work and subsequent uncommitted stages. It does not itself interrupt an active committed step. The human or ordinary individual System AI can separately issue a supported stop, cancel or retask instruction; physical conditions may also interrupt work.

A · Change what starts next

Reorder waiting work and uncommitted stages. The active step continues unless a separate supported stop, cancel or retask instruction is issued, or physical conditions interrupt it.

B · Also interrupt active work

A higher-priority eligible job normally causes the owning controller to request a supported interruption. It starts only when the necessary resources or equipment become available.

Selected: A. Completing a step does not lock in priority for the rest of its job. Separate stop/retask commands follow activity-specific recovery rules; they do not instantly free teams, move cargo, refund consumed resources or take another System's commitments. AI may react immediately while physical stopping takes time. Continuous power/coolant allocation remains separate.

AD02 · Selected A

Shared Console facts for humans and individual System AIs

Every System AI on a Console may read its complete permitted blackboard, just as the human operating that Console gets all that knowledge. A policy uses the relevant information; it does not receive a separately restricted subset.

If radar combines legitimate observations from several producers, the Console's AIs may use that same combined picture. Each AI still makes its own decisions and retains its own policy memory. Producers must filter hidden world information before publication.

Cross-Console information still requires explicit sharing or Coordination. Showing two tabs at one Station does not automatically pool their blackboards. Knowledge access does not grant another System's control authority. Hiding an AI-operated entry does not turn off its observations or data publication.

AD12 · Selected A The working view follows the Console

The incoming operator inherits radar zoom, presentation filters and the information record being inspected. The layout adapts to their device; personal accessibility settings remain their own. Operational targets and commands stay with the Systems, while permitted knowledge and history stay scoped to the Console.

Handoff preserves work context without resetting operation, pooling another Console's knowledge or introducing special Backfill behaviour. Deliberate Viewscreen publication keeps its existing rules; this choice alone does not start or transfer a publication. Exact storage and replication remain to be designed.

AD34 · Selected A Common metadata, typed observations and reports

Sensor readings, repair findings and shared intelligence share identity, subject, provenance and observation/receipt timing conventions, with confidence or uncertainty where applicable. Each domain retains its own typed contents and interpretation. Console views and individual System AI reuse history, copying and deduplication support.

A probe reading forwarded through Science to Tactical retains its original age and evidence lineage. Copying it does not count as a new independent observation.

Current dossier evidence and scan records provide separate groundwork. A common contract is proposed; the dossier's existing subject/provenance/text deduplication rule is not sufficient for repeated measurements.

These are observations and structured reports, distinct from physical truth, commands and private Scenario progression. Claims may conflict or be wrong. Receiving Consoles retain their own receipt times and permitted scope; contact references cannot reveal undiscovered identities. Free-text chat does not automatically become structured evidence.

Exact schemas, correlation, fusion, retention and storage remain open. Shared metadata requires neither one global database nor a new network protocol. Automatic feeds, knowledge limits and human/AI parity retain their selected rules.

AD35 · Selected A Shared Console knowledge processors

Reusable typed processors derive combined contact, status and other supported views from the Console's permitted records. Humans and individual System AIs read the same assessments and can inspect their supporting evidence. Producers own acquisition; Console processing owns the derived view.

Keep domain-specific rules: position estimation and repair-report updates share metadata and history support without using one universal combination formula.

Workshop binds supported processors and typed inputs/outputs as Console data. This is ordinary data-oriented processing with per-Console knowledge scope, not a new AI controller. Current sensor publication and JavaScript Console builders provide integration points; the proposed common pipeline is not yet implemented.

Retain the distinction between source evidence and derived estimates, and show unresolved contradictions. Processing cannot reveal hidden facts, infer intentions as truth, choose targets or broaden sharing. Humans and ordinary System AI interpret significance and choose actions. Association rules, scheduling, retention and projections remain open; existing AI reading time and networking remain in force.

AD03 · Selected A

Physical parts provide reusable mechanisms

A physical part is a condition/repair target assigned to a compartment, without precise internal coordinates. It can supply or affect several reusable mechanism components. A capacitor assembly can provide capacity, energy intake and thermal behaviour while remaining one repair target. AD44 assigns HP to its compartment; damage there applies component status effects rather than reducing a separate part HP pool.

Workshop shows the physical part and its capabilities together. Typed bindings describe how part conditions change effective operation. This does not prescribe inheritance or an ECS object tree.

AD04 · Selected with clarification Shared services and mounts have different boundaries

A receiving System has a link component for its shared service. A coolant conduit can be damaged independently of the source or other receiving links. A central pump has its own System when its output needs independent control. Shared capacity is counted once.

The System boundary follows shared command ownership. A turret receives a target and whether to attack, jam, or both against that target. Its mounted capabilities use those channels inside one System. A pump component could also be included, sharing the System's on/off toggle. Components need not all perform the same kind of work.

Workshop must expose source and receiving-link failures separately and reject cross-System turret references. A shared service does not duplicate capacity, and a composite System does not add an independent AI/controller for every mechanism.

AD13 · Selected A Workshop composes supported mechanisms

Engine modules implement the mechanism library. Workshop data chooses components, parameters, connections, command bindings and presentation. New equipment combinations need no new code; genuinely new mechanisms extend the code library.

AD14 · User-selected direction Configurable AI state trees
