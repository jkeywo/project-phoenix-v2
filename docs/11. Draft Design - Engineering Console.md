# Engineering Console

## Overview

Engineering manages power distribution across Helm, Weapons, and Science consoles, and monitors the ship's auxiliary battery. Engineering is also the only console that can see which systems are damaged and need repair.

## Power Allocation UI

- Increment (+) and decrement (−) buttons per console (Helm, Weapons, Science).
- Each console shows its current power level (1–4).
- Total allocated points displayed alongside battery charge level.
- The + button is disabled when total allocated points = 8 (6 base + 2 aux) or the console is at 4.
- The − button is disabled when a console is at 1.
- Buttons are fully locked during battery exhaustion (see below).

## Battery

- The auxiliary battery provides up to 2 extra power points beyond the 6 base points.
- Charge/discharge rate is determined by a lookup table indexed by total allocated points (3–8):

```toml
[battery]
capacity = 100.0
# Index 0 = 3 total points, index 5 = 8 total points
# Positive = recharge/second, negative = discharge/second
rates = [6, 5, 4, 2, -2, -6]
emergency_threshold = 25.0  # % charge required before control is restored after exhaustion
```

- Allocating fewer than 6 base points results in idle power, which accelerates recharge (lower index = faster recharge in the rate table).

## Battery Exhaustion

- When battery charge reaches 0:
  - All consoles are forced to 1 power point each (emergency power).
  - Engineering's buttons are locked — no reallocation possible.
  - Battery recharges at the rate for 3 total allocated points (maximum recharge rate).
  - Once charge reaches `emergency_threshold` (e.g. 25%), Engineering regains full control.

## Repair

- Engineering is the only console that sees which systems are damaged and require repair.
- Each player console (Helm, Weapons, Science) has its own repair button on their respective UI.
- Engineering must communicate to the relevant operator (via voice) to press their repair button.
- Pressing a console's repair button starts a repair cycle: hull HP restored over time at `repair_rate × power_multiplier` for that console, then enters cooldown.
- Repair rate and cooldown are configurable in the ship entity TOML.

## Configurable Values (in ship entity config)

- Power multiplier tables per console (`[helm.power_multipliers]`, `[weapons.power_multipliers]`, `[science.power_multipliers]`)
- Battery capacity, rate table, emergency threshold (`[battery]`)
- Hull repair rate and cooldown

All numbers should be configurable in the ship entity TOML.
