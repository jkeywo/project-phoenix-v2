## Effects, counterplay and evidence

The existing finite EMP receipt, automatic expiry and resistance accumulation
remain authoritative. Shields, physical obstruction, range/arc, target evidence,
equipment conditions, reserve/heat and existing EMP receiver policy provide
counterplay. SignalProtection only suppresses its declared electronic
interference effects; it is not silently extended into physical EMP immunity.
Shared own System status shows actual EMP separately from destruction. The
attacker receives ordinary impact/effect reports permitted by existing evidence,
never a private enemy component census or guaranteed remote disablement result.

Workshop uses the actual shared weapon, energy, ownership/Console and interior
editors, accepted source/history/includes and Preview/Test validation. No new
effect schema or duplicate EMP processor is justified by this configuration.

Required evidence includes the accepted optional recipe preserving base seats,
systems, ratings and resource rules; real client builder/component/semantic
action reachability at the hosted Comms Console; Human bank selection/fire/stop
and ordinary AI operation through existing admission; positive paid physical
EMP and failed shield/geometry/unfunded cases; original condition/resistance
expiry and restored operation without a repair ritual. Continue actual finite
work/EMP through fresh JSON for at least 360 complete captures/digests, with
malformed ownership/payload/condition candidates refusing before writes.

Runtime, authoring and full-host source proof are separate from native execution,
device/browser journeys, balance/performance and crew workload acceptance.
Broader selected information/access operations remain open where they need a
separate supported objective and delivery/prerequisite policy. This example does
not invent those policies or close all of increment 17.
