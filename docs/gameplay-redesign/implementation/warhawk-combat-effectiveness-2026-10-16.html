Stop avoiding radar history

The Cruiser's long withdrawal was driven by false collision hazards. Historical radar contacts were being treated as growing solid obstacles.

The cause

Seed 3 began firing at 1.92 seconds. Its first attack ended around 12.03 seconds, then it did not restart its own beams until 108.58 seconds. Separation grew to about 346 units despite a correct current target observation, usable steering and healthy local energy. Red Alert remained active through most of the withdrawal; Shadow was a consequence of the long firing gap.

One lost tactical contact's uncertainty radius grew from 108 to 278 units between seconds 20 and 40. Helm projected that uncertainty as a dangerous obstacle. Reconstructing the published geometry gives collision urgency around 0.60, matching the Cruiser's imminent-collision threshold. The shared planner repeatedly overrode the combat orbit with escape steering, while lateral thrust pushed away too.

Both mechanisms needed correction: measured Helm views now mark predicted history non-dangerous, and steering avoidance respects that flag just as shared hazard assessment already did. No hidden world lookup or extra knowledge is introduced.

The hazard reconstruction uses published samples and ordinary JavaScript trigonometry, so it is diagnostic evidence rather than a bit-exact replay of a simulation tick.

What changed

  • Keep lost tracks, positions, identities and uncertainty in the permitted knowledge view.
  • Exclude predicted records from automatic collision avoidance. Current information-only contacts remain hazards even when they cannot be selected for control.
  • Retain charted-obstacle avoidance and the existing physical collision simulation.
  • Advance simulation rules to 0.78; protocol 46 and snapshot format 89 are unchanged.

Weapon coefficients, shield and hull values, alert policy, defeat accounting and thresholds are unchanged. The accepted optimisation implementation is retained.

This policy applies to every predicted contact, including a temporarily lost lone contact. It does not guarantee collision safety. Fresh duplicate contacts remain separate observations; contact fusion is outside this fix.
