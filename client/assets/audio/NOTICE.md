# Private feedback audio

`private_refused.wav`, `private_timeout.wav`, and `private_alert.wav` are original
non-speech sine tones generated by `scripts/build-private-audio.mjs` for Phoenix.
They use the repository's license. The descending refusal, separated timeout,
and rising actionable alert distinguish the three outcomes without dialogue.
The existing `ui_click.ogg` is retained for routine controls and the output test.
Conservative per-cue levels and the coalescing interval are authored in
`private-feedback.json`; Master and category mutes multiply those levels.
