
Engineering should be able to adjust the ship's power between
- Helm - affects movement values, and the speed of their repair button
- Weapons - affects damage, and the speed of their repair button
- Science - affects shields, and the speed of their repair button

The ship has 6 power points to distribute.
- A console can have between 1 and 4 assigned.
- 2 points preserves the normal values for that console.

Engineering have an auxiliary power battery.
- This allows for up to 2 more power points to be assigned.
- Each of these assigned points drain the battery.
- It takes 60 seconds for 1 extra point to drain the battery.
- It takes 20 seconds if both points are assigned.
- It takes 60 seconds to fully recharge the battery.

All the numbers in this file should be configurable.
