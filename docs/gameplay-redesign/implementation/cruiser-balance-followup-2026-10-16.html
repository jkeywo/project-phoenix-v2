Cruiser balance: fights can now reach victory

5/20 wins on integrated main. The corrected baseline had zero. Fresh seeds also produced five wins. This is a measured improvement; the existing balance gate remains red.

What changed

Retain contact
Tactical radar 50 → 300, matching existing torpedo reach. Helm uses its existing permitted feed.
Make shared evidence visible
Helm shows off-scale contacts as outward rim chevrons, preserving its close-range scale and uncertainty rules.
Deposit damage locally
Standard beam penetration 20 → 10. Less internal reach, more damage to the struck compartment. Both banks and default fitting agree.

Beam DPS remains 32 per bank. Weapon ranges, firing cycles, shields, compartment HP, victory rules and all matrix thresholds are unchanged in this tuning pass. The earlier missing-fact, focus, fixed-arc and victory corrections were merged first at aff0fad9; tuning was then merged at 25ccf11e. Concurrent optimisation commits are preserved.
